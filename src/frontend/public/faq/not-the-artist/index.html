Is Lil Blunt the game the same as the music artist?

No. Lil Blunt: The Smoke Realm is a free 2D platformer game you play in a web browser. It is not a musician, and it is not affiliated with any recording artist who shares the name — the name simply collides.

What this page is

The phrase "Lil Blunt" is used both by a recording artist and by this video game, so a search for it returns a mix of results and it is easy to land on the wrong one. This page exists to disambiguate: if you were looking for music, this is not it, and if you were looking for the game, you are in the right place. There is no shared ownership, no partnership, and no endorsement in either direction. The overlap is a naming coincidence, nothing more.

What the game actually is

Lil Blunt: The Smoke Realm is a side-scrolling arcade platformer built in Godot 4.3 and exported to HTML5, so it runs directly in a desktop browser over WebGL2 with no install and no download. You play as Lil Blunt, a green outlaw prospector working the Dustrock Mines of a Wild West town, running and jumping deeper into the mine while dodging hazards and keeping a score. The recurring antagonist is the Tax Man, who turns up to take a cut of what you dig. It is free, there is nothing to buy, and no crypto wallet or account is required to play.

The controls are fixed keyboard bindings: the Left and Right arrow keys move, Spacebar jumps, Enter throws axes, Shift sprints, and K performs a burst dash. WASD is not bound, which is the most common reason a first-time player reports the character will not move. Score accrues from collected pickups and defeated enemies as you descend; each run ends when your lives are spent, and your best runs are posted to a leaderboard styled as an old-west wanted board. The entries shown on the site today are demonstration data, labelled DEMO on the page.

Who "Lil Blunt" is in this game

Within the game, Lil Blunt is a fictional character and mascot — a bright green frontier outlaw, drawn as a personified cannabis leaf, who works a mining claim in the Smoke Realm. The character is the game's own creation. He is not a depiction of, a reference to, or a stand-in for any real person or performer. Any resemblance of the name to a music act is incidental.

Music and audio

The game ships with its own background soundtrack and sound effects. It does not use, sample, cover, or license any track from a recording artist named Lil Blunt or from anyone else under that name. If you arrived here expecting songs, streams, or an album, this project has none of those — it is a game, and its only audio is the score written for it.

How to be sure you are in the right place

The game lives at two stable addresses: the official website at smokegame.win, served from a canister on the Internet Computer, and a free hosted build on itch.io that runs the same HTML5 export inside an iframe. Both describe a browser platformer, show gameplay screenshots of a Wild West mine, and list the keyboard controls — move with the Left and Right arrow keys, jump with Spacebar, throw axes with Enter. If the page you are reading talks about mining the Dustrock Mines, the Tax Man, arrow-key platforming, and a wanted-board leaderboard, it is this game. If it talks about song releases, streaming links, or a discography, it is the unrelated artist and is not connected to this project.
