What the game is

Lil Blunt: The Smoke Realm is a free 2D side-scrolling platformer that runs in a web browser. You play a green outlaw prospector digging through the Dustrock Mines of an 1800s Wild West mining town, chasing a high score while dodging hazards and the Tax Man.

Read the full about page → — covers the setting, the character, how it differs from other browser platformers, and the three connected protocols.

How to play

Left and Right arrow keys to move, Up arrow to jump, Enter to throw axes. Get as deep into the mine as you can before your lives run out. It is free, runs in a browser, and needs no account to start.

Read the full how-to-play guide → — controls, scoring, the wanted board, and a frequently asked questions section.

Getting started with Internet Identity

Signing in is optional. If you choose to, you use Internet Identity — the Internet Computer's login system — which authenticates through your device's fingerprint reader, face scan, or hardware key. There is no password, no seed phrase, no wallet to install, and no fee.

You can play the entire game without signing in at all. See how to play for the details.

What is live today

The website is genuinely served from a canister on the Internet Computer, and Internet Identity sign-in genuinely works. The leaderboard and ecosystem figures shown on the site are demonstration data and are labelled DEMO — a live on-chain leaderboard with real player scores has not shipped yet.

The three protocols

The Smoke Realm is the shared setting for three related projects: SMOKE (cannabis-green haze and lounge interiors), DIAMONDS (the 1800s mining town and crystal-blue veins), and GOLD (the Wild West gold rush).
