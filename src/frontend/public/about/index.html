About Lil Blunt: The Smoke Realm

Lil Blunt: The Smoke Realm is a free 2D platformer game you play in your web browser. You control Lil Blunt, a green outlaw prospector, running and jumping through the Dustrock Mines of an 1800s Wild West mining town. Your high scores are recorded on the Internet Computer blockchain.

What kind of game is it?

It is a side-scrolling arcade platformer — the same basic genre as classic run-and-jump games. You move left and right, jump over gaps and mine carts, avoid hazards, and try to get as deep into the mine as you can before you run out of lives. Each run is scored, and your best runs are posted to a leaderboard styled as an old-west "wanted board."

There is no purchase required and no wallet needed to simply play. It runs in a normal browser.

Who is Lil Blunt?

Lil Blunt is the game's main character and mascot: a bright green frontier outlaw who works a mining claim in the Smoke Realm. The tone is deliberately laid-back and comedic rather than gritty — the recurring antagonist is the Tax Man, who turns up to take a cut of what you dig. The game's tagline, "you can't tax the vibe," sets the register.

What does the blockchain part actually do?

This is the part most people want explained plainly, so here it is without jargon.

The game runs on the Internet Computer (often abbreviated ICP), a public blockchain that can host entire web applications rather than just financial transactions. The site you are reading is itself served from the Internet Computer.

You can optionally sign in using Internet Identity, which is the Internet Computer's built-in login system. It works through your device's existing security — a fingerprint reader, a face scan, or a hardware key — rather than a password or a seed phrase. When you sign in this way, the scores you earn get written to the chain, so the leaderboard is publicly verifiable rather than sitting in a private company database.

You do not need to buy anything, connect an existing crypto wallet, or pay a transaction fee to do this. If you would rather not sign in at all, the game still plays normally — you just won't have a persistent on-chain record.

How is it different from other browser platformers?

  • Verifiable scores. Most browser-game leaderboards are a database row that the operator can change. Here the record lives on a public chain.
  • No wallet friction. The usual barrier to a "Web3 game" is installing a wallet extension and funding it. Internet Identity removes that step — you sign in with the biometrics already on your device.
  • The whole site is on-chain. Not just the scores. The front end itself is served from a canister on the Internet Computer, not from a conventional web host.
  • It is a real game first. The platforming works on its own terms; the chain integration is a feature, not the entire premise.

The three protocols

The Smoke Realm is the shared setting for three related projects, each with its own site and its own visual identity:

  • SMOKE — the cannabis-culture side of the world, all green haze and lounge interiors. lilblunt.win
  • DIAMONDS — the 1800s mining town, lantern-lit shafts, and crystal-blue veins in the rock. diamonds1111.win
  • GOLD — the Wild West gold rush, dusty saloon boards, and ore by the ton. mine4gold.app
