Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This site does not ask for your name, your email address, or a password, and it has no account to sign up for. What it does collect is an anonymous visit identifier and a record of which advert or link brought you here. This page explains exactly what that means.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the website at https://www.smokegame.win/ only. The game itself is hosted on itch.io; when you click through to play, itch.io's own privacy policy applies to that visit, alongside the game's own terms.

What is collected

What is never collected

  • Your name, email address, postal address, or phone number.
  • Any text you type. There is no contact form, comment box, or search field that is recorded.
  • Passwords — the site has none.
  • Payment or card details. Nothing on this site is for sale.
  • Biometric data. If you sign in with Internet Identity, your device performs the fingerprint or face check locally; that data never reaches this site.
  • Uploaded files. There is no upload feature.

Cookies

This site sets no tracking cookies. The anonymous visit id and the campaign tags are kept in your browser's local storage, which is not transmitted automatically the way a cookie is. Clearing site data in your browser erases both immediately.

Who the data is shared with

  • PostHog — our product analytics provider, which receives the anonymous id, the pages viewed, and the campaign tags. Data is processed on servers in the United States.
  • The Internet Computer — this website is served from a canister on the Internet Computer, a public blockchain network. As with any web host, your request reaches the network's boundary nodes.

Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with advertisers or data brokers.

How long it is kept

Analytics events are retained by our analytics provider under their standard retention. The identifiers held in your own browser persist until you clear your site data.

Removing your data

Because nothing here is tied to a name or an email, most of it is under your direct control:

  • Clear it yourself, immediately: clearing this site's data in your browser deletes the anonymous id and the campaign tags. A later visit simply generates a new random id with no link to the old one.
  • Opt out of analytics: a browser that blocks analytics requests, or the "do not track"/tracker-blocking features in most modern browsers, prevents the events being sent at all. The site works normally either way.

This is a decentralised Web3 project. There is no company, no head office, and no support desk holding a file on you — which is also why erasure here is something you perform directly rather than request. The analytics records that do exist carry no name, no email, and nothing that identifies you, so once the identifier in your browser is cleared there is nothing left that points back to you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, POPIA, or the CCPA — including rights to access, correct, or erase data about you and to object to its processing. The design above is intended to satisfy those by default: the only identifiers are anonymous, they live in your own browser, and you can destroy them at any time without asking anyone. We do not sell personal information, and we hold nothing that could identify you to sell.

Children

This site is not directed at children. The game is presented as 21+ entertainment and should not be used by anyone below the age of majority where they live.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above changes with it.
