How to Play

Lil Blunt: The Smoke Realm is free, runs in your browser, and needs no account to start. Open it, use the arrow keys, and try to get deep into the mine without losing your lives.

Controls

ActionKeys
Move leftLeft arrow, or A
Move rightRight arrow, or D
JumpSpacebar, or Up arrow

The goal

Get as deep into the Dustrock Mines as you can. Jump the mine carts, dodge the hazards, and keep your score climbing. Every run is a fresh claim — when you lose your lives, you start over and try to beat your own best.

Your strongest runs land on the wanted board, the game's leaderboard, styled after an old-west bounty poster. The entries shown on the site today are demonstration data, labelled DEMO on the page — see below.

Signing in is optional

You can play the whole game without an account. You can also sign in with Internet Identity — the Internet Computer's login system, which uses your device's fingerprint reader, face scan, or a hardware key instead of a password or seed phrase.

There is nothing to buy and no fee. See the about page for a fuller plain-English explanation.

What is live today

Being straight about this, because it is a fair question to ask of any Web3 project: the website is genuinely served from the Internet Computer, and Internet Identity sign-in genuinely works. The leaderboard and ecosystem numbers currently on the site are demonstration data and are labelled DEMO where they appear — a live on-chain leaderboard carrying real player scores has not shipped yet.

There is also no token reward, airdrop, or NFT minting attached to playing. Proof of Play is an achievement layer, not a payout.

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes — completely. No purchase, no wallet funding, no transaction fee.

Do I need a crypto wallet?

No. Most "Web3 games" ask you to install a wallet extension and fund it before you can do anything. This one doesn't. Play with no account at all, or sign in with the biometrics already on your device.

Do I earn tokens or NFTs by playing?

No. There is no token payout, airdrop, NFT minting, or play-to-earn mechanic. Proof of Play is an achievement layer, not a reward programme.

Does it work on mobile?

The game is built for a browser with a keyboard, so a desktop or laptop is the intended experience.

What is ICP?

ICP is short for the Internet Computer, a public blockchain that hosts complete web applications rather than only handling transactions. This website is itself served from it.
