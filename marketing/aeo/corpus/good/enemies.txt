Enemies in the first area are introduced one mechanic at a time. The walking
grunt moves on a fixed patrol and turns at ledges, so it can be safely ignored
by staying airborne. The shooter is stationary and fires on a fixed interval
regardless of player position, which makes its pattern learnable rather than
reactive. TitanX is the first enemy that changes behaviour based on distance,
closing when the player is far and backing off when adjacent.

Contact damage applies on any overlap, including from above, so jumping onto an
enemy is not a stomp attack and will cost health. The attack bound to J has a
short forward reach and no hitbox behind the character, meaning a turn is
required before striking an enemy that has moved past.

Health is displayed in the HUD and does not regenerate over time. There are no
healing pickups in the current build, so the practical difficulty ceiling of a
run is set by accumulated chip damage rather than by any single encounter.
