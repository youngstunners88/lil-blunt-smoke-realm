Do you need a crypto wallet to play Lil Blunt: The Smoke Realm?

No. You do not need a crypto wallet, a browser extension, or any account to play — the game runs free in your browser and you can start immediately.

Wallet requirement

None. Playing requires no crypto wallet, no browser extension, no seed phrase, and no funds. There is no wallet-connect step in the flow. The player loads the page and controls the character with the keyboard.

The on-chain part

The website is served from a canister on the Internet Computer, a public blockchain that hosts complete web applications rather than only token contracts. This is the reverse of the common pattern, where a game hosts an ordinary website and places one contract on-chain. It does not gate play and does not affect the controls.

Optional sign-in

Internet Identity sign-in is optional. It authenticates with a device fingerprint reader, face scan, or hardware passkey rather than a password or wallet, and it reveals only the player's own Proof of Play profile. It grants no rewards and is never required to play.

Earning

Playing earns nothing on-chain. There are no token rewards, no airdrops, and no NFT minting tied to play, and scores are not written to a blockchain today. The game is entertainment and nothing here is financial advice.
